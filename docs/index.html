Prophet Model Implementation for Digital Payment Volume Forecasting with Holiday and Event Effects

We forecast daily UPI transaction volume with Prophet and test how much holidays and events improve the forecast. NPCI publishes UPI data monthly only, so the daily series is synthetic, built from real NPCI monthly totals (Jan 2022 to Aug 2026) with effects of known size.

ATSA Group 2: Rohan Dedhia, Duncan D'lima, Sanaz Katpitia
